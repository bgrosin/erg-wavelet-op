# Pipeline overview

Raw ERG -> zero-phase sharp IIR notch (line frequency, Q = 35) -> zero-phase
4th-order Butterworth high-pass (60 Hz) -> parallel analysis:

* CWT path: real Morlet ('morl'), 512 log-spaced scales from
  `getWaveletScales(fs)`; energy-calibrated PSD = K * time-mean |W|^2 with K
  from Parseval calibration (`CalibrateWaveletPSD`, cached per grid by
  `getWaveletPSDConstant`); outputs peak PSD (PPSD) and dominant frequency.
* STFT path: spectrogram (60 Hz resolution display) + Welch PSD; PPSD and
  dominant frequency on the zero-padded grid.

Validation: synthetic OP-like Gabor bursts embedded in AR(8) surrogates of
each cohort's own pre-stimulus noise, amplitude-matched to the cohort's
filtered OP amplitude (Family A: single bursts 80-115 Hz; Family B: paired
bursts 15 ms apart; SNR sweep at fixed 1000 Hz).
